### Evolutionary information for RNA secondary-structure prediction
- **Supervisors:** Brian Parker and Jiayu Wen, John Curtin School of Medical Research, ANU
- Integrated conserved RNA structure information into a GAN-based secondary-structure prediction workflow.
- Focused on connecting sequence-derived evolutionary signals with machine learning models for RNA structure prediction.

### Molecular cloning and functional studies of F-type lectin from roughskin sculpin
- **Supervisor:** Dr. Shanshan Yu, Marine College, Shandong University
- Studied a fucose-binding lectin from *Trachidermus fasciatus* involved in antibacterial defense.
- Conducted gene cloning, sequence analysis, structural prediction, tissue distribution analysis, expression profiling, stimulus-response analysis, and in-vitro bioactivity tests.
- Co-authored one manuscript currently under review.

### Antimicrobial peptide gene identification in *Rapana venosa*
- **Supervisor:** Dr. Shanshan Yu, Marine College, Shandong University
- Identified and cloned an antimicrobial peptide gene.
- Applied bioinformatics methods for structure prediction, functional-domain analysis, and phylogenetic analysis with MEGA.

### Plant diversity survey and ecology study in Weihai
- **Supervisor:** Prof. Zhao Hong, Marine College, Shandong University
- Conducted field surveys on plant diversity, distribution, and abundance in the mountains of Weihai.
- Documented more than 70 species and prepared over 50 plant specimens.

### Coastal animal diversity study at Golden Beach, Weihai
- **Supervisor:** Dr. Liu Xu, Marine College, Shandong University
- Collected more than 40 marine organism specimens.
- Provided baseline data for local marine biodiversity research and resource conservation.
