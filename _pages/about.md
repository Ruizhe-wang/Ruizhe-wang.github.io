Hi, I am **Ruizhe Wang** (王睿哲). I recently completed my Master of Science in Quantitative Biology and Bioinformatics at the **Australian National University**, building a strong foundation across computational methods and the biological sciences.

My work centers on **AI for science** — using machine learning to make sense of complex biological data. I am currently focused on **representation learning**, developing **dimension-reduction** methods that uncover meaningful structure in high-dimensional datasets. I am especially interested in how good representations can support biological discovery, structural prediction, and the analysis of sequence-derived signals.

During my master's research, I worked on integrating evolutionary information into **RNA secondary-structure prediction** using generative adversarial networks. Earlier, I contributed to molecular and functional studies of marine lectins and antimicrobial peptides, including cloning, sequence analysis, structural prediction, expression profiling, and functional annotation.

I am continuing to strengthen my background in machine learning and structural bioinformatics, and I am actively looking for research opportunities where I can contribute to AI-for-science and computational biology projects.
