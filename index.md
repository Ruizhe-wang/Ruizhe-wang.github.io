## AI for Science

I am Ruizhe Wang (王睿哲), an MSc graduate in Quantitative Biology and Bioinformatics from the Australian National University. I work on **AI for science** — building machine learning methods that turn complex biological data into understanding.

My current focus is **representation learning**: I am developing **dimension-reduction** approaches that reveal meaningful structure in high-dimensional biological data. More broadly, I am interested in how learned representations can advance RNA structure prediction, structural bioinformatics, and data-driven scientific discovery.

I am currently looking for research opportunities where I can contribute to AI-for-science and computational biology projects and continue developing as a researcher.

## Research Focus

- **Representation learning** — dimension-reduction methods that uncover structure in high-dimensional biological data.
- **AI for science** — machine learning and deep learning applied to scientific, especially biological, problems.
- **Structural bioinformatics** — learned and physical approaches to RNA secondary-structure prediction and sequence analysis.
- **Molecular and functional biology** — gene cloning, expression profiling, structural prediction, and bioactivity analysis.

## Selected Work

**Representation learning for high-dimensional biological data**

Developing dimension-reduction methods to learn compact, informative representations that expose structure in complex biological datasets.

**RNA secondary-structure prediction with evolutionary information**

Integrated conserved RNA structure information into a GAN-based prediction workflow during MSc research at ANU.

**F-type lectin characterization in roughskin sculpin**

Contributed to molecular characterization and functional analysis of an antibacterial fucose-binding lectin from *Trachidermus fasciatus*.

**Antimicrobial peptide gene analysis in *Rapana venosa***

Identified and analyzed an antimicrobial peptide gene using cloning, structure prediction, domain analysis, and phylogenetic methods.
